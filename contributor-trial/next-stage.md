# Conditions for a featured launch

1. Pin and reproduce a Kira 3 or FIRE 7 baseline on the same integrals and basis.
   Provide a prebuilt, screened local/container environment so installation does
   not consume the donor's entire session. The present trial used neither solver.
2. Add at least one genuinely multi-loop CPU-scale family, including numerator
   targets, with exact proof export
   or reconstructed symbolic identities checked by a separate implementation.
   A small two-loop vacuum/sunset or modest propagator family is a candidate;
   actual completion and checking costs must be measured before assigning it.
3. Commission an independent newcomer run, with no prior harness familiarity,
   and an independent physics/checker review. Measure time to first valid result.
4. Prepare a fresh evaluation pool and a ladder of open tasks, rather than allowing
   repeated tuning against these now-exposed examples. Include a cached/tightly-seeded
   comparator and disclose all failed restrictions and fallbacks.
5. Set maintainer review capacity and a public coordination channel before intake.
   The owner has consented to maintain, but no public listing or consent exists.

These are launch gates, not prerequisites for local experimentation. They determine
whether the project is an honest science pilot rather than a solver-optimization demo.
